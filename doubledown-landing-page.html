What DoubleDown delivers

Turn fragmented records into trusted entity data

DoubleDown applies configurable selection rules — survivorship — to create one trusted, "golden" record for each entity, on a scalable Snowflake foundation for operations, analytics, and downstream workflows.

Entity resolution that fits your context

Deterministic and probabilistic matching with survivorship to create trusted golden records.

Confidence you can explain

Stewardship and lineage for defensible match decisions — every merge is traceable back to its source.

A reusable foundation

Onboard new entity sources without restarting the process. Built once, extended repeatedly.

Applied in the field

Public health environments

A public health agency · Cross-system entity resolution

Challenge

The agency needed to connect fragmented data across immunization, patient index, and disease surveillance systems — matching people, providers, and organizations while protecting PII and PHI.

Deloitte solution

DoubleDown resolved records through configurable matching, survivorship, and stewardship workflows, with a UI for search, review, merge, and unmerge, plus governance and data-sharing capabilities.

Impact

A more trusted cross-system view of key entities, improved interoperability, and stronger data sharing and analytics across authorized stakeholders.
